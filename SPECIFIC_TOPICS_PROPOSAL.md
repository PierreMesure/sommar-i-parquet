# Proposed specific topics

This is an editorial proposal based on a manual review of all 200 raw clusters
in `data/nlp/topics.parquet`. Luna labels were used as a reading aid, but the
list below was selected and phrased manually from the keywords, representative
excerpts, and episode coverage.

The parenthetical raw topic IDs are **provenance only**. They should not be
used as a direct mapping: several raw clusters mix concepts, and the final
episode assignments should instead be calculated against definitions and
examples for the curated topics.

The section headings below are only for making this proposal readable. They
are not the future broad-theme hierarchy.

## Raw clusters intentionally not promoted

The following clusters are not proposed as user-facing topics in their current
form:

- **Generic personal narrative:** 0, 2, 3, 7, 10, 12, 22, 27, 33, 37, 47, 65, 66, 76, 85, 98, 113, 160, 169.
- **Names or characters rather than a reusable subject:** 8, 32, 39, 68, 82.
- **Mixed clusters without a coherent promise to users:** 6, 17, 18, 40, 52, 57, 60, 67, 71, 93, 96, 100, 106, 107, 125, 143, 146, 151, 159, 166, 181, 187, 199. Useful concepts found inside these clusters have been retained above, but membership must be reassigned semantically.
- **Song lyrics, greetings, seasonal framing, or transcription artifacts:** 11, 103, 111, 120, 166, 169, 196.

Some IDs appear both as rejected clusters and as provenance for a curated
topic. That is deliberate: a mixed cluster can contain good evidence without
being safe to expose as a topic or to copy wholesale.
